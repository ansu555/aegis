Yours alone

Your wallet is a smart contract on its own NEAR account. At the end of setup the account's key is deleted, so only your card and your phone, together, can move the funds. No company, Aegis included, can.

Already in your pocket

No hardware wallet to buy, charge or lose. The chip on your bank card already holds a key that can't be copied out.

No seed phrase

Nothing to write down and nothing to steal. Add a backup card you keep at home, or a guardian phone you trust, and recover on a new phone.

Security & reliability

A card alone never moves funds.

The wallet contract checks your bank card's RSA signature and your phone's P-256 signature on-chain, every time.

Two keys, both required

Every transfer needs your card and a fingerprint-unlocked phone key. Neither one can pay alone.

Keys that can't be copied

The card's key is made by your bank inside its EMV chip. The phone's key lives in the Android Keystore's secure hardware and can't be exported.

Limits and delays on-chain

Payments above your instant limit wait a delay you can cancel from either key. The rules live in the contract, not in the app.

Freeze in one tap

Lost your card or phone? Either key on its own can freeze the wallet. Unfreezing needs both.

Ready for the day something goes wrong.

Losing a card or a phone shouldn't mean losing your money.

  • Lost card. Freeze, then remove it with your backup card and your phone. A new card takes its place.
  • Lost phone. Recover on a new phone with your cards. Your old phone can cancel the recovery during its 24-hour wait if it wasn't you.
  • Guardian phone. Someone you trust can cancel and freeze, and co-sign together with a card.
  • Inheritance. Name an heir. If you're inactive for the period you choose, they can claim. Every check-in or payment resets the clock.

Questions

Does tapping my card charge it or tell my bank?

No. Aegis asks the chip for the same kind of signature a payment terminal uses to check that a card is genuine. No PIN is entered, no payment is made, and nothing is sent to your bank or the card network. The chip's own transaction counter does go up by one, as with any contactless tap.

Can someone with a reader in a crowd use my card?

Not on its own. A card signature never moves funds without your phone's fingerprint approval. And the card signs a challenge taken from a block after the payment was recorded, so a signature skimmed earlier is useless.

What if I lose my card?

Freeze the wallet from your phone, then remove the lost card using your backup card. If you have no backup card yet, add one: one is enough, and it should stay at home.

What if I lose my phone?

Install Aegis on a new phone and start a recovery with your cards. Recovery waits 24 hours, so your old phone or a guardian can cancel it if it wasn't you.

Is my card number stored or sent anywhere?

No. The card number, track data and cardholder name stay inside the tap session in the phone's memory and are never logged, stored or sent. The app keeps only the last four digits to show you which card is which.

Which cards work?

Contactless EMV bank cards. Aegis supports the three ways cards prove they're genuine: Visa fDDA, Mastercard CDA and classic DDA. Settings → Check a card tells you whether yours can sign before you set anything up.

Will Aegis keep working if the team disappears?

Yes. Your wallet is a contract on your own NEAR account, and the app talks to public NEAR RPCs. There is no Aegis server in the path, and the code is open source.

Can I use it with real money today?

Not yet. Aegis is on NEAR testnet. Mainnet opens after testing with real bank cards and an independent contract review.
